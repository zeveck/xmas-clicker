# Brief: Chief Toy Officer (a remake of Xmas Clicker)

**In one sentence:** a cozy Christmas clicker where you hammer toys together
in Santa's workshop, hire elves to help, and climb from Assistant Junior Toy
Maker to Chief Toy Officer before Christmas Eve.

**The feeling:** warm, silly and satisfying. Candlelight, sawdust and
cocoa; every tap goes *tok!*, every finished toy pops, and the tree under
the window fills up with presents. The jokes live in the job titles.

## What they asked for

The request was "remake this, with lots of freedom", so the list is what
the 2017 original clearly aimed at, plus the person's words:

- [ ] Remake https://synapticnoise.com/xmasclicker, leeway **lots**: build what it was reaching for.
- [ ] Click a hammer to build the selected toy; a progress bar per toy.
- [ ] Ten toys, unlocked one by one: Wooden Tractor, Yo-yo, Puzzle Cube, Wooden Sword, Sketch An Etch, Fidget Spinner, Chess, Super Game System, Bike, Smartphone.
- [ ] A counter for each toy made.
- [ ] Prestige, and ten rank titles, word for word, from Assistant Junior Toy Maker to Chief Toy Officer.
- [ ] Four hammers to upgrade through: Cheap, Heavy, Supreme, and a legendary fourth (Thor's Hammer in the original).
- [ ] Toys have a value (never used in the original): they should earn something to spend.
- [ ] A Christmas tree, and a pile of presents (both empty in the original).
- [ ] Sean's prototype ideas: hire elves who build on their own, elf hammers, click upgrades.
- [ ] Christmas-card lettering (Mountains of Christmas).
- [ ] Zero-padded counters (a nod).
- [ ] Fix: the top-rank crash, the half-built console, the toy order; drop the ctrl-click cheat.
- [ ] `--plan-only` and `--share-design`: a reviewed plan, shared as a page with mock pictures.

## Who, where, how long

Families and anyone at Christmas; the original was a family project (Sean's
prototype, "ryan-bike"). Desktop and phones (portrait). Sessions of a few
minutes, coming back over the season; about 20–30 minutes of active play
from first tap to Chief Toy Officer, then it keeps going.

## Style

No `--style` given. The original used Christmas-card fonts, a candy-stripe
bar and a tree silhouette. Aim: a painted storybook workshop, warm and
handmade, with chunky, readable toy icons. Genre reference: idle clickers
in general (tap, buy helpers, numbers go up, a lucky bonus now and then);
borrow the loop, not any game's look, names or items.

## Decisions made for the person

- New name, **Chief Toy Officer** (the top rank, and the joke of the
  original), with "a remake of Xmas Clicker" on the title. Easy to change.
- All art replaced: the original's images are clip art and product photos
  from the web, in clashing styles, and can't be published safely.
- "Thor's Hammer" becomes the **Thunder Mallet**, "Super Game System" (a
  real console's photo) becomes the **Pixel Play** console, and "Sketch An
  Etch" (a real toy's look) becomes the **Doodle Dial**, each an original
  design. The other toys keep their names.
- Toys earn **cookies** (the elves' pay), spent on hammers and helpers.
- Becoming Chief Toy Officer brings Christmas Eve: an ending, then free play.
